Open a suite for what the task is and what every variant does.

static The task exactly as RoboCasa365 ships it. dynamic Something the task depends on moves in a steady, extrapolatable way from the first tick. nuisance The objects the task needs stay put; an added object that has nothing to do with the task moves instead. reactivity A one-shot event that cannot be extrapolated from what came before.

Perturbation standard

How big every perturbation is, in numbers a reader can compare across tasks and policies (docs/task_suite.md §10, decided 2026-10-13). Each suite page prints the numbers next to its cells.

Nominal timeEach suite declares the reference policy's median completion time on the static task (GR00T N1.5, sync, kitchen (1, 1), seeds 0..9). Every task-relative number below is in units of it.
1. Travel, not speedA drift level is the distance the target covers during one nominal completion: 10, 20, 40 cm for an object, 25, 50, 100 % of the joint range for a fixture. The speed is derived per task.
2. Keep-outThe nuisance object is placed at least 15 cm from the task's item and its destination and from the straight path between them, so it can never physically interfere; nuisance and dynamic share the same levels.
3. Outcome-calibrated impulsesA topple declares what it must do (moved by at least 5 cm, still on its surface) and the force is tuned per item category to that: a sweep over every kitchen and seed under hold, the 90th percentile of the per-seed smallest forces that meet it; a category not seen in the sweep gets the fallback, the median of the calibrated ones. The preview records whether it was met.
4. Progress triggersReactivity fires when the gripper comes within 10 cm of the item or at half the nominal time, whichever first, so it fires for every policy.
5. SwitchesA switch fires at a fraction of nominal time and must switch to a goal that is not already satisfied; seed-dependent instructions use cases. A cell whose switched goal was already true is flagged.
6. Admission6a: a task is admitted when the best policy of the reference set (GR00T N1.5, π0.5, π0) succeeds at 15 % or more on its static variant in sync mode. 6b: a variant cell is admitted when it passes thirteen scene predicates under the hold action, no policy (loads, fires, goal_open, switch_open, stays_up, separated, no_penetration, in_view, in_reach, settles, outcome_met, fixture_moves, fixture_still); a one-shot on a fixture joint is fired from the state it fires in, the joint driven open robot-free first. 6c: every admitted cell counts in success and variant cost; timing columns are reported only where a policy's sync success is at least 50 %.
