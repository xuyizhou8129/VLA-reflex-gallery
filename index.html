Task suite gallery

27 RoboCasa365 tasks in 2 tracks. Each task is a suite of variants that start from one stored initial state and differ only in what moves or changes, when and by how much. A suite's page states every variant's levels and trigger and shows a clip of every cell.

Under review: the unit tasks' D1 and D3 interrupts

The unit tasks are the atomic tasks (no stages). Their interrupts of D1 (where the item is) and D3 (where the destination is) are under review, each in its three forms: continuous (a drift from the first tick), before (a slide as the hand nears the item) and during (a slide at the grasp's or the place's onset). Each entry links the variant's section and gives, per kitchen, how many of its levels are admitted (rule 6b, the admission decisions and the trigger gate applied). The control, D0 (something the task does not use drifts by the same amounts), is on each page; D4 and D5 (the fixture and goal-switch variants) are parked and folded at the end of each page.
